# 6. Offboarding Module in HCM — Full Enterprise Features
The **Offboarding** module manages the complete employee exit lifecycle, from resignation or termination initiation until final settlement, access removal, asset clearance, document archiving, payroll closure, and employee record finalization.
In a proper HCM/ERP system, Offboarding is not just "mark employee as terminated." It must coordinate multiple departments:
* HR
* Payroll
* Finance
* IT
* Asset Management
* Administration / Facilities
* Security
* Legal / Compliance
* Line Manager
* Knowledge Transfer Owner
* Employee Relations
* Document Management
This module is especially important because employee exit affects salary, benefits, company assets, system access, legal compliance, financial liabilities, and audit risk.
---
# 1. Purpose of Offboarding Module
The purpose of the Offboarding module is to ensure that employee exit is controlled, compliant, documented, and financially accurate.
## Main objectives
* Manage resignation requests
* Manage termination processes
* Manage retirement, end of contract, and other exit types
* Apply notice period rules
* Trigger approval workflow
* Create exit checklist
* Collect company assets
* Remove system access
* Complete clearance process
* Calculate final payroll
* Calculate end-of-service benefits
* Process pending loans, advances, deductions, and expenses
* Conduct exit interview
* Transfer knowledge and handover tasks
* Archive employee documents
* Close employee employment record
* Preserve historical data for reporting and audit
## Standard offboarding flow
```text
Exit Initiated
      ↓
Exit Reason / Type Selected
      ↓
Approval Workflow
      ↓
Notice Period / Last Working Day Confirmation
      ↓
Offboarding Checklist Generated
      ↓
Knowledge Transfer / Handover
      ↓
Asset Return / Clearance
      ↓
Access Removal
      ↓
Final Payroll / Settlement
      ↓
Exit Interview
      ↓
Employee Status Updated
      ↓
Documents Archived
      ↓
Offboarding Completed
```

# 48. Important Validation Rules
The system should validate:
* Employee must be active before offboarding starts
* Exit type is required
* Exit reason is required
* Last working day is required
* Last working day cannot be before resignation date unless authorized
* Termination must have approval before employee status is finalized
* Employee cannot have two active offboarding cases
* Final settlement cannot be approved before payroll inputs are finalized
* Mandatory clearance must be completed before settlement payment unless waived
* Asset deduction must be approved before payroll deduction
* Access removal must be confirmed before case completion
* Position vacancy must be updated after employee exit
* Employee cannot submit future leave after last working day
* Attendance records after last working day should be blocked
* Employee cannot be deleted after exit
* Terminated employee should be archived, not removed
* Rehire eligibility must be defined before case completion, if required by policy
* Offboarding reversal must require high-level approval

# 49. Common Mistakes to Avoid
## 1. Only changing employee status to terminated
Offboarding is a full process, not just a status change.
## 2. No integration with payroll
Final salary, leave encashment, loans, and deductions must be connected to payroll.
## 3. Weak IT access removal
Failure to remove access creates serious security risk.
## 4. No asset clearance
Missing laptops, phones, cards, and tools can create financial loss.
## 5. No knowledge transfer
Work disruption happens when exit handover is not tracked.
## 6. No final settlement workflow
Final payment should not be made without clearance and approval.
## 7. Deleting terminated employees
Never delete employees with historical transactions. Archive them.
## 8. No exit reason analytics
Without exit analytics, management cannot understand attrition problems.
## 9. No confidentiality control
Termination reasons, legal notes, and exit interviews must have restricted access.
## 10. No audit trail
Exit decisions, approvals, settlement, and access removal must be traceable.
